Our story

Fewer things. Better things.

Synth started as a simple idea: a wardrobe should be calm, not cluttered. We design a small range of essentials in a tight monochrome palette, so every piece works with the rest of your wardrobe.

We choose organic and recycled fibres where we can, work with small production runs, and only restock what people actually wear.

Timeless design

Clean cuts and neutral colours that outlast trends.

Better materials

Organic cotton, recycled wool blends and durable denim.

Small batches

Less waste, and more care in every piece.

Delivery & returns
